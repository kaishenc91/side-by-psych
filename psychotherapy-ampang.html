Individual psychotherapy

A professional space to understand what you are going through.

People come to therapy for many reasons. You do not need a diagnosis, or a perfectly clear explanation of what is wrong, before making an appointment.

When life feels heavy

Therapy can support people living with anxiety, low mood, stress, emotional exhaustion, grief, loneliness, self-criticism or difficult transitions.

When patterns keep repeating

You may notice the same conflict, shutdown, worry, people-pleasing or painful relationship pattern returning despite your best efforts.

When you want to know yourself better

Some clients come with a wish to understand their emotions, needs, boundaries and relationships more clearly.

What sessions are like

Therapy begins with a conversation, not a test.

The first session is an opportunity to share what has brought you here, ask practical questions and see whether the service feels suitable. The pace is collaborative. You will not be expected to disclose more than you are ready to share.

My main therapeutic orientation is Emotion Focused Therapy (EFT). It offers a humanistic way of listening to emotions and the meanings they carry. Depending on your needs and the clinical context, I may also draw from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, mindfulness and creative expressive approaches.

The aim is not to remove every difficult feeling. It is to make room to understand what those feelings may be telling you, and to find more workable ways of responding to yourself and the situations you face.

Frequently asked questions

Before your first appointment.

Can I attend an in-person therapy session after work?

Yes. Face-to-face appointments at the Ampang centre are available daily up to 10.00pm, subject to appointment availability. This may suit people who prefer an in-person therapeutic space outside usual working hours.

Do I need a referral or diagnosis?

No. You may contact the centre directly to ask about availability and whether the service may be a suitable next step for you.

Is therapy confidential?

Sessions are handled with professional confidentiality, within the usual ethical and legal limits, including situations involving serious risk of harm, safeguarding concerns or legal requirements.

Is this an emergency service?

No. Side by Psych Wellness Centre is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or may harm yourself or someone else, please contact emergency services or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
