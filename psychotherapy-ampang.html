Starting therapy

Do I need a diagnosis to start therapy?

No. Many people come because they feel stuck, overwhelmed, self-critical, anxious, low, or unsure how to make sense of what they are feeling. You can also seek support to understand yourself and your relationships better.

What happens in the first session?

The first session usually involves getting to know you, understanding what brings you to therapy, and discussing what you hope will change. Your psychologist will explain the approach, invite your questions, and begin exploring what matters most to you.

You don’t need to prepare a perfect explanation or tell your whole story at once.

Will my sessions and personal information remain confidential?

Your privacy is an important part of our work. Information about your sessions is not routinely shared with family members, partners, employers, or others without your consent.

Confidentiality has ethical and legal limits, including certain serious safety concerns and legal requirements. Your psychologist will explain these limits and how your information is handled at the beginning of therapy. Where possible and appropriate, any necessary disclosure will be discussed with you.

You’re welcome to ask about privacy before booking or at any point during therapy.

How often should I attend, and how many sessions will I need?

There is no single number that suits everyone. The frequency and length of therapy depend on your concerns, goals, circumstances, and progress. You and your psychologist can discuss an initial plan and review whether further sessions would be useful.

A four-session package is a payment option; it does not mean that therapy must be completed within four sessions.

Understanding Emotion Focused Therapy

What is Emotion Focused Therapy (EFT)?

EFT helps you understand your emotions and work through feelings that leave you stuck. With your psychologist’s support, you explore what your feelings tell you about your needs and develop more helpful ways of responding to yourself and others.

The work can involve experiencing and exploring emotions, managing overwhelm, and developing new emotional responses within a collaborative therapeutic relationship.

What concerns might EFT help with?

EFT may help with depression, anxiety, difficulties following painful experiences, low self-esteem, inner conflict, and unresolved relationship feelings.

Whether it suits you depends on your needs, preferences, and current circumstances. Some situations call for other support before deeper emotional work.

What if I find it difficult to understand or talk about my feelings?

You do not need to arrive knowing exactly what you feel. Your psychologist can help you gradually explore your experiences and find words for them. You can also discuss any doubts about focusing on emotions.

If something feels difficult to share, you can say so, ask for a pause, or let your psychologist know that you are not ready to explore it.

Online therapy

Who might benefit from online therapy?

Online therapy may suit people who live far from a therapist, find travelling difficult, or feel more comfortable opening up from their own space. Some people with social anxiety may find video sessions less intimidating.

Suitability depends on your needs, preferences, and access to a private space.

What are the advantages and limitations of online therapy?

Online sessions remove the need to travel and allow you to attend from familiar surroundings. However, some people find video sessions less personally connected, and technical problems or interruptions may affect the session.

You will need reliable internet and a quiet, private space. Some exercises require extra preparation, and in-person support may suit you better if staying emotionally connected during video sessions is difficult.

What do I need for an online therapy session?

A little preparation helps you focus on the conversation:

  • A safe, quiet, private space where you can speak without interruptions or being overheard. Ask others not to enter, and keep your screen out of view.
  • A stable, private internet connection. Avoid public Wi-Fi.
  • A charged computer, tablet, or phone with a working camera, microphone, and speakers or headphones. Test them before the session and keep your device steady.
  • Comfortable seating, clear lighting on your face, and notifications silenced.

Your psychologist also needs a private, quiet space. Headphones can help with privacy and sound quality, but cannot stop others hearing your own voice.

Do both of us need to keep our cameras on?

Yes. At Side by Psych, both the client and psychologist keep their cameras on during online sessions so we can see each other clearly.

Position your camera so your face is visible, with light in front of you rather than a bright window behind you.

If using a camera is difficult or raises concerns for you, please discuss this before your appointment so we can consider a suitable arrangement.

What if I cannot get privacy, or the connection drops?

Please tell us before your appointment if you may be interrupted, overheard, or unable to use video reliably. We can discuss whether a different time, a more private location, or an in-person session would be more suitable.

Agree a backup contact method with your psychologist before starting. If someone enters the room or you feel privacy is lost, pause the conversation and let your psychologist know. If the call drops, use the agreed contact method to decide the next step.

Fees and practical arrangements

How much are the session fees?

We aim to keep therapy affordable, with clear, upfront fees:

  • In-person individual psychotherapy: RM150 per session.
  • Online individual psychotherapy: RM100 per session.

Your first session includes intake and usually lasts 90 minutes or longer, at the same session price. There is no separate intake fee or extra charge for the additional time.

Follow-up sessions last at least 60 minutes. Four-session packages start at RM500 in person and RM360 online.

Separate psychological assessments, reports, and other professional services are quoted in advance, so you can understand the costs before deciding to proceed.

When should I make payment, and what is the cancellation policy?

Please make payment directly to Side by Psych Wellness Centre and send your payment receipt via WhatsApp to +60 11-1112 3703 at least 24 hours before your appointment.

If you need to cancel, please let us know at least 12 hours in advance. Cancellations made with less than 12 hours’ notice will be charged the full session fee.

For prepaid packages, a cancellation with less than 12 hours’ notice counts as one used session, with no additional cancellation fee.

Can I attend after work?

Face-to-face appointments at the Ampang centre are available daily up to 10pm, subject to availability. All sessions require an appointment. Please contact us to discuss a suitable time.

Can I contact the centre during a crisis?

The centre is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services, go to the nearest hospital emergency department, or reach out to a crisis helpline.
