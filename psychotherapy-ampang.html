Individual psychotherapy

Begin with
where you are.

You don't need a diagnosis or a clear explanation of what's wrong to make an appointment. Therapy can begin with what feels difficult, or with a wish to understand yourself better.

We can explore anxiety, low mood, stress, grief, loneliness, self-criticism, relationship difficulties, and life transitions.

Your first session

Let's get
to know you.

The first session is a chance to share what brought you here, ask questions, and consider whether this service feels suitable. You won't be expected to disclose more than you are ready to share.

My work is primarily guided by Emotion Focused Therapy. Depending on your needs, I may also draw from ACT, mindfulness, and creative expressive approaches.

We work together to understand your experiences and explore more workable ways of responding.

A few practical questions

Before your
appointment.

Can I attend after work?

Yes. Face-to-face appointments in Ampang are available daily up to 10pm, subject to appointment availability.

Do I need a referral or diagnosis?

No. You may contact the centre directly to ask about availability and whether the service may be suitable for you.

Is therapy confidential?

Sessions are handled with professional confidentiality, within the usual ethical and legal limits, including serious risk of harm, safeguarding concerns, or legal requirements.

Is this an emergency service?

No. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services, go to the nearest hospital emergency department, or reach out to a crisis helpline.
