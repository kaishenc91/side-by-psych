Website & WhatsApp privacy

A small amount of data, handled with care.

This notice explains the Side by Psych website, its contact options and the restricted Side by Psych OS Test WhatsApp app described below. Clinical records and psychotherapy confidentiality are covered separately.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What this website collects

The website itself does not create an account for you and does not store the information typed into the enquiry form on a website server. When you choose “Send via WhatsApp” or “Send via Email”, the website prepares a message and opens the relevant app on your device. The message is only sent when you choose to send it there.

Please avoid including highly sensitive health information in an initial website, WhatsApp or email enquiry. These channels are not intended for emergencies.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, the website uses Google Analytics to understand basic use of the site, such as which page was viewed, broad device information, general geographic area and whether a contact or language link was selected. This helps us improve information that visitors find useful.

Measurement is off until you opt in. You may allow analytics only, or also allow Google Ads measurement. With ad measurement enabled, Google receives a conversion event when you select a WhatsApp, telephone or email contact link, or use the enquiry form to open a contact app. These events represent contact attempts, not confirmed messages, appointments or treatment outcomes. Measurement cookies and advertising click identifiers help attribute contact attempts to ads.

We do not send form answers, your name, telephone number, email address, message, selected support area or clinical information through our contact events. Ad personalization, remarketing and enhanced conversions are not enabled by this tracking code. Google processes measurement data under its own privacy terms. You can decline measurement without affecting access to the website or appointments. Use “Privacy choices” at the bottom of any page to change your choice.

Contacting the centre

If you contact Side by Psych through WhatsApp, email, telephone or another external service, that service's privacy terms also apply. Information needed to respond to an enquiry or arrange an appointment will be handled for that purpose.

Side by Psych OS Test: WhatsApp administration

Restricted test — 17 October 2026: this uses a separate Meta test number and the centre owner’s approved test recipient. It is not open for client messages. A separately authorised FAQ rehearsal may send up to three automatic website-information replies within a maximum 30-minute window, only to the owner’s test recipient in response to new, clearly fictional messages. Information and purpose. The test uses the owner’s WhatsApp number, clearly fictional message text, message identifiers and timestamps to check delivery, receiving and duplicate handling. Information comes from the approved participant and Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. During account setup, Meta also shares the Facebook name and profile picture authorised by the owner. Please do not send client details, health information, payment information or identity documents to the test number.

Storage and providers. The prepared local receiver, once activated, is designed to save only marked fictional text from that approved participant and test account, with its message identifier and timestamp, in encrypted local test records on the centre’s Mac mini for owner review. It does not save contact profile names, attachments or complete incoming platform notifications. It does not import existing client chats or access clinical session notes. Meta processes WhatsApp messages and account information under its own service terms and may process information outside Malaysia. For this temporary test, Cloudflare Quick Tunnel is the proposed service carrying HTTPS traffic between Meta and the Mac. If approved and activated, Cloudflare will handle test traffic and network metadata; processing may occur outside Malaysia. The test connection does not keep receiving while the Mac is offline. Local storage does not mean all message processing takes place on the Mac.

Automation and AI. The WhatsApp FAQ rehearsal has ended. A separately approved administrative AI rehearsal allows the owner to request up to three suggestions within 30 minutes using only a clearly fictional question, selected language, approved public website FAQ text and its version identifiers, and drafting instructions. These are sent to OpenAI through its API to prepare a draft for owner review; processing may occur outside Malaysia. No phone numbers, client records, attachments, clinical notes or chat history are added. Each request needs the owner’s exact-content review. The rehearsal does not send WhatsApp messages or confirm appointments, payments or receipts. OpenAI API data is not used for model training by default. Response storage is disabled, but OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs, including content, for up to 30 days, with exceptions under its policies. Zero Data Retention is not claimed. Encrypted local requests and results remain available for owner review; provider-held records follow OpenAI’s policies. This is not approval to process real client enquiries with AI.

Retention and your choices. Participation in this restricted test is voluntary. The owner will review local test records when connection testing ends and delete copies no longer needed for testing or resolving a reported issue. Limited technical evidence may be retained without message text. You may request access, correction or deletion, ask about retention, or withdraw from further testing by contacting hello@sidebypsych.com or +60 11-1112 3703. We may verify your identity before acting. Withdrawing from testing does not affect access to the centre’s ordinary services. Records held by Meta remain subject to its own services and policies.
